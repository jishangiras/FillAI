# FillAI Branding

FillAI should feel clear, private, and fast: a simple tool that turns natural language into completed forms.

## Name

Use **FillAI** everywhere user-facing.

Avoid:

- Fill AI
- FillAi
- FillAI AI
- SmartFill

## Logo

Primary mark: `public/icons/fillai-icon.svg`

The mark combines:

- A form/document motif for form filling
- A check mark for reviewed completion
- A small sparkle for AI assistance
- Blue/teal colors for trust, speed, and privacy

## Colors

- Primary blue: `#0C4A8A`
- Primary teal: `#0F766E`
- Soft cyan: `#E0F2FE`
- Success mint: `#A7F3D0`
- Text: `#111827`
- Muted text: `#6B7280`

## Voice

Use short, direct labels:

- Fill Form with AI
- Speak to fill
- Your defaults
- Smart matching
- Support development

Avoid model names or implementation details in the default UI.
